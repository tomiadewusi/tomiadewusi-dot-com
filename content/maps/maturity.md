Every note carries a `maturity` field in its frontmatter, rather than being treated as equally authoritative:

- **seed** — a captured idea that is still exploratory.
- **developing** — actively being expanded and refined.
- **stable** — mature enough to cite or build upon.
- **deprecated** — retained for historical context, superseded by a better understanding.

Nothing is deleted just because thinking evolved — deprecated notes stay, because how understanding changed over time is worth keeping visible.
